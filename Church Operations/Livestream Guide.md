# Livestream Guide

> **Key info:** Livestream starts automatically at 10 AM via ProPresenter. You just need to schedule it during the week.

---

## Weekly Prep (5 min)

Do this anytime before Sunday:

1. Open [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/channel/UCyU7upEfTQI8xVKt1KG1fUQ/livestreaming/manage)
2. Click **Create** then **Go live**
3. Select **Reuse settings** (copies last week's setup)
4. Update the title with the correct Sunday date
5. Set the time to 10:00 AM and click **Done**

That's it! ProPresenter handles the rest automatically.

---

## On Sunday

**You don't need to do anything special for the livestream.**

- ProPresenter's calendar feature auto-starts encoding at 10 AM
- YouTube catches the stream and goes live
- The final slide in ProPresenter ends the stream

**Your only job:** Click the first worship slide right at 10:00 AM — this switches the camera feed for livestream viewers.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Stream not starting | Check YouTube Studio — is the event scheduled? Is it set for today? |
| Stream ended early | Someone clicked the final dismissal slide too soon |
| No video on stream | ProPresenter output not routing to encoder — check preferences |
| Poor video quality | Close other apps, check network connection |
