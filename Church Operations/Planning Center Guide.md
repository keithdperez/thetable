## For Volunteers

### Check Your Schedule

1. Download the **Church Center** app (iOS/Android)
2. Search for "The Table Church Lemont"
3. Sign in with your email
4. Tap **My Schedule** to see when you're serving

### Confirm or Decline

When you get a scheduling request:
- Open the email or app notification
- Tap **Accept** or **Decline**
- If declining, add a note so leaders can plan accordingly

**Please respond within 48 hours** — it helps the team plan!

### Adding Songs to a Plan

1. Go to [Services](https://services.planningcenteronline.com)
2. Select the upcoming service plan
3. Click **Add Item** and search for the song
4. Drag to reorder the setlist

### Scheduling Team Members

1. Open the service plan
2. Click the team position you need to fill
3. Search for the person and click **Schedule**
4. They'll get an email to accept or decline
