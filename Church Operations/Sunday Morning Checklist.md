## Setup (arrive by 9:00 AM)

### Media (5 min)

- [ ] Log into Mac Mini (password: TableChurch102)
- [ ] Turn ON confidence monitor (TV behind booth)
- [ ] Turn ON audience TVs (x2) — press Input button, select "AV Receiver"
- [ ] Open ProPresenter — verify slides are loaded and camera preview shows

> **TV not showing slides?** Press the physical Input button on the TV, then select "AV Receiver"

### Audio (10 min)

- [ ] Connect phone to "The Table" WiFi
- [ ] Turn ON amp (booth cabinet)
- [ ] Turn ON stage monitors (x2, switches on front of each)
- [ ] Open Mixing Station app — search for XR18 mixer
- [ ] Put fresh batteries in all wireless mics
- [ ] Unmute bass channel (prevents amp buzz)

> **Can't find mixer?** Make sure you're on "The Table" WiFi, not "Living Room"

### Stage (5 min)

- [ ] Turn ON keyboard (Roland RD88)
- [ ] Turn ON bass amp (Mark Bass)
- [ ] Turn ON drums (Roland VAD504)
- [ ] Acoustic guitar: plug Xvive transmitter into guitar, check receiver is on

### Sound Check

- [ ] Check each mic — aim for green-yellow on meters (~-15 dB)
- [ ] Walk the stage — check musician monitor mixes
- [ ] Listen from the room — adjust house mix as needed

**Aux Routing:**
- Aux 1+2 = In-ears
- Aux 3+4 = Floor monitors

## Shutdown

### Audio

- [ ] Mute bass channel (before turning off amp — prevents buzz)
- [ ] Turn OFF stage monitors

- [ ] Put mic batteries on charger

### Stage

- [ ] Turn OFF keyboard, bass amp, drums
- [ ] Acoustic guitar: power off Xvive, return paddle to charger

### Media

- [ ] Quit ProPresenter
- [ ] Turn OFF all TVs
- [ ] Lock Mac Mini (Control + Command + Q)

## Quick Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| No sound from mains | Check amp is ON (booth cabinet) |
| Bass amp buzzing | Unmute the bass channel in Mixing Station |
| TV shows wrong input | Press Input button on TV, select "AV Receiver" |
| Can't find XR18 mixer | Verify phone is on "The Table" WiFi |
| Wireless guitar cutting out | Check Xvive battery, ensure receiver connected |
