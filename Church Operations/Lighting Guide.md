# Lighting Guide

> **Key info:** Lights change automatically during the service. ProPresenter sends MIDI cues to LightKey.

---

## How It Works

**You don't need to touch the lights during a normal service.**

1. ProPresenter slides have MIDI cues embedded
2. When you click a slide, it sends a signal to LightKey
3. LightKey changes the stage lighting to match

For example: clicking the first worship slide turns on stage lights and dims house lights.

---

## Manual Override

If you need to control lights manually (testing, special events, etc.):

1. Open **LightKey** app on the Mac Mini
2. Use the scene buttons to switch lighting looks
3. Use faders to adjust individual fixtures

**Common scenes:**
- House lights (bright, for setup/teardown)
- Worship (stage lit, house dim)
- Message (even lighting, less dramatic)

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Lights not changing with slides | Check LightKey is running on Mac Mini |
| Wrong lighting scene | Open LightKey, manually select correct scene |
| Lights stuck | Quit and reopen LightKey |
