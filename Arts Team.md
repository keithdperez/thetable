# Arts Team – Rhythm & Roles

_A shared space to plan, dream, and support each other in worship and AV leadership at The Table._

---

## 🛠️ Areas of Focus

### AV
- Sunday tech prep (slides, livestream, audio routing)
- Midweek fixes (cables, power, backups)
- Volunteers: training, roles, troubleshooting
- Smart stage power / remote control

### Worship
- Setlist planning for Sunday
- Flow/movement ideas (keys, pad, prayer, scripture moments)
- Introducing new songs
- Volunteer encouragement & rotation
- Live vs. background pads
- Vocal stacks (who sings what?)

### Scheduling
- Plan ahead 4–6 weeks in Planning Center
- Block key weekends (vacations, guest worship, etc.)
- Schedule midweek rehearsals (when needed)

### Upgrades
- Wishlist of gear (keyboard, mics, lights, etc.)
- Sound improvement (monitor mixes, livestream audio)
- Stage layout updates
- Volunteer tech training (ProPresenter, ATEM, etc.)

### Dreaming Ahead
- Worship nights (seasonal or quarterly)
- Guest musicians / collaborative sets
- Youth/Student worship integration
- Recording live sets or writing new songs

## 🌱 Monday Morning Rhythm (at church)

**Time:** 9:30–11:00am (flexible based on week)  
**Purpose:** Pray, plan, prepare for the week in unity.

### Weekly Flow:
- Open with short prayer + any heart check-ins  
- Look at the upcoming Sunday  
- Talk through possible setlist ideas  
- Assign keys/vocals/instrumentation  
- Review Planning Center schedule  
- Tech check: what needs prep/fixes this week  
- Dream ahead: upcoming events or ideas  
- End with encouragement or small shared prayer

---

## 🎯 Expectations

- We show up as partners, not just helpers
- We check in relationally, not just operationally
- We prepare worship with spiritual intention, not just musical excellence
- We aim to leave Monday mornings feeling more connected, less burdened
